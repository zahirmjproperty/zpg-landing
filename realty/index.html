Inside the module

One deal. One record. No surprises.

Built as an interactive prototype with 26 working screens — not a slide deck. Every stage of a deal is written down as it happens, so the agent, the client and the office are always looking at the same file. The figures on the prototype screens are sample data.

Agent Registry & Hierarchy

REN tag, insurance, level and reporting line for every negotiator. The management upline is a tree, sorted by chain or by production — so it is always clear who answers for which deal.

Who may act, who answers ↗

Deal Pipeline

Every listing and every deal on one board: what stage it is at, what is blocking it, and what has to happen next. Nothing advances on a missing document or an unsigned page.

Nothing moves half-done ↗

Document Generator

Type the deal once — the system numbers the letter, computes the statutory fee and files the PDF. The closing letter carries a 12-item payment schedule and the full document chain from the appointment letter.

Numbered, computed, filed ↗

Send & Track Signatures

A link per party, a status board and reminders. Clients sign on their phone, soft copy for both sides, and the stamping clock starts by itself once the last signature lands.

The clock starts itself ↗

Deals & Documents

Search every deal, filter by month, export the file. The complete paper trail for any transaction sits in one place — so a client asking "what did I agree to?" gets an answer, not a search.

The whole file, one search ↗

Compliance Guard

Who is allowed to act, who may not be brought in, and what must be disclosed — checked on every deal. The engine refuses a configuration that breaches Act 242 or Act 500, and there is no override switch in the interface.

The law, enforced in code ↗

What the engine refuses

Hard stops, not warnings.

Each rule refuses the action and writes the attempt to the audit log, so a rejected step can be shown to a regulator or to BOVAEP exactly as it was attempted.

Gate

Only registered agents may act

An unregistered person cannot be brought into a deal. Act 242 permits an estate agent or negotiator to act only with a valid REN/PEA registration — deal through anyone else and the client is left with no recourse.

Registration is checked before the name enters the file.

Gate

No agent on both sides

A negotiator may not act for the buyer and the seller on the same deal. The system refuses the pairing — a conflict of interest is not something to manage after the fact, it is something to prevent.

One negotiator, one side.

Gate

No payment for recruiting

Bringing a person into the agency is not a payable event. A structure that pays for recruitment rather than for completed deals is what Act 500 s.27B treats as a pyramid scheme — so the system will not record one.

Every ringgit traces to a completed deal.

Gate

Nothing is edited in place

Every change to a deal records who made it and when. A disputed transaction can be reconstructed exactly as it happened — which is what protects the client, the agent and the agency alike.

Every action on every document, in order.
