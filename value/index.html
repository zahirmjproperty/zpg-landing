Inside the module

Four ways to value the same property. One defensible answer.

Built for Malaysian practice — where the number has to survive a bank's review, an auditor's question and a client who disagrees. So the workings are kept, not just the result.

Comparable Selection

Rank market records against the subject on area, type, tenure and age, adjust them to a common basis, and keep the selection as the reason the number moved.

Evidence, not a guess ↗

Residual & HBU

Work the site back from gross development value, less construction, fees and finance, to the land value a developer can actually pay — and test the highest and best use.

Land, from the top down ↗

Income & DCF

Capitalise net income, or discount the cash flow year by year with a yield you can justify. Built for tenanted and income-producing assets, not only vacant ones.

Yield and cash flow ↗

Cost (DRC)

Depreciated replacement cost for the properties the market does not trade — schools, places of worship, specialised buildings — where comparables do not exist.

Review & Freeze

A valuation moves through draft, review and approval. The reviewer must pick the figure, the author cannot approve their own work, and an approval locks the record from further edits.

How it keeps you honest

The system refuses the shortcuts.

These are enforced rules, not good intentions. Each one blocks a step rather than warning about it afterwards.

Gate

Three verified comparables, or no report

A valuation cannot produce a report with fewer than three verified market records. Asking prices are held separately and never count as evidence.

Every record carries its category, so a listing is never silently treated as a transaction.

Gate

The basis of value must be supported

State a basis the engine does not implement — a fair value under a reporting standard, for instance — and it is rejected rather than quietly answered with a market figure.

No number is better than a number wearing the wrong label.

Gate

You cannot approve your own valuation

The author of a valuation cannot approve it, and that applies to administrators too. The reviewer must select the figure under review and record a reason when it sits outside the indicated range.

Once approved, the record freezes — no edit, no resubmit, no reject.

Gate

An approved figure does not move

Approval seals the valuation. A later transaction, however attractive, cannot retroactively change the figure that was signed off.

Revisions happen as new records, so the history stays readable.
