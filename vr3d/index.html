One connected ecosystem

A tour does not stand alone. Neither should your data.

Access

Viewing is open. Uploading is not.

Anyone can open a published tour — send the link and it works, with no account and no sign-in. Media intake is the exception: submitting photos or video for a new tour is an operator action, and that is the step being put behind a ZENTRA ID.
